Frequently Asked Questions

Is ClipFlow affiliated with TikTok?

No. ClipFlow uses the official TikTok API but is an independent application.

Do I need to authorize my account?

Yes. Every TikTok account must authorize access through OAuth.

Does ClipFlow store my TikTok password?

No. Authentication is performed exclusively through TikTok OAuth.
